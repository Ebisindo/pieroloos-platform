# Session 05 — Engagement & Activity Management Engine

## Lifecycle
INTAKE → ASSESSMENT → PLANNING → APPROVAL → EXECUTION → VERIFICATION → RECORDING → CLOSED

An engagement status is a domain state. It must not be changed arbitrarily by the UI.

## Activities
CREATED, UPDATED, REVIEWED, APPROVED, COMPLETED, COMMENTED, STATUS_CHANGED, DOCUMENT_ATTACHED.

Activities are append-oriented operational history. They are not the final security audit ledger.

## Authority
PieroloOS prepares, records, routes and surfaces workflow events. Human decision authority remains explicit.

Future expansion:
tasks, assignments, deadlines, notifications, approvals, evidence links, automation rules, background jobs and client-portal activity.
