# PieroloOS — Session 09: Compliance Intelligence & Operational Control Center

Session 08 created the Compliance Obligation Register.

Session 09 turns that register into an operational control system:

Compliance obligations
→ deadline intelligence
→ priority classification
→ portfolio health
→ alerts
→ escalation decisions
→ notification queue boundary
→ Command Center visibility.

## Professional-grade controls

### Portfolio intelligence
Derives total, compliant, due-soon, overdue, blocked, evidence-pending and review-pending obligations, completion percentage and portfolio status.

### Deadline intelligence
Uses persisted obligation dates. It does not invent jurisdictional requirements.

### Escalation
Escalation is policy-driven and traceable through level, reason and lifecycle.

### Notifications
Notifications are durable records behind a dispatcher boundary. A background worker/provider can be added later.

### Human authority
The system surfaces operational conditions. It does not make legal, tax or regulatory conclusions. Professional review remains explicit.
